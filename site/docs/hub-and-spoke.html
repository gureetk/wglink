Hub and spoke

By default every member of a network connects to every other. With a hub, members connect only to the hub, which passes traffic between them. This helps when only one machine can be reached from everywhere, such as a VPS, or for laptops and phones on the road:

wglink connect vps laptop phone --name road --hub vps

Spokes send the whole network's traffic to the hub. The hub turns on forwarding for its WireGuard interface alone when that comes up, through a PostUp line in its config, so nothing else on the machine changes.
